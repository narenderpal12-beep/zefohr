Build Phase 1 only: marketing, organisation signup and subscription administration. Do not implement operational HRMS suites as part of this phase.

**Why:** The user explicitly reserved the separate multi-module HRMS app for Phase 2.

**How to apply:** Future work on marketing should distinguish planned suite capabilities from working Phase 1 features; do not turn the marketing request into HRMS module development.

Do not mention competitor names anywhere on the public site. Keep the mandatory compliance disclaimer editable and visible in both Why Us and the footer.

**Why:** The user explicitly required this to avoid overstating what software alone fulfils.

**How to apply:** Preserve the disclaimer's meaning when redesigning or editing copy: EOR, POSH, FRRO and labour-law support require appropriate legal, statutory and operational expertise; software alone does not fulfil every obligation.
